Clinical Research & Past Cases

Planned evidence connectors. The items below are architecture demonstrations, not live research findings.

NOT LIVE · NO DATABASE CONNECTION
PubMed / MEDLINE

Planned: retrieve research publications relevant to the reported symptoms, use verifiable citations, and show publication dates.

Evidence-based clinical guidelines

Planned: retrieve current guidance from approved authorities, validate freshness and scope, and clearly separate guidelines from individual diagnosis.

Published case reports and case series

Planned: identify potentially relevant reports, note selection bias, and never present a similar case as evidence of confirmed diagnosis.

Patient-specific medical history

Planned: with consent, retrieve authorized clinical records using secure integrations and identify contradictory or missing information.

Design rule: Research results will only be displayed when a source has actually been retrieved. The Version 1 demo does not invent citations or clinical evidence.

Multi-Agent Product Roadmap

Designed to integrate specialist agents while preserving physician authority.

VERSION 1 · CURRENT PROTOTYPE

SymptomDetect AI™

Multilingual symptom intake, optional camera, local document preview, evidence architecture, demo safety signals and visit summary.

VERSION 2 · PLANNED

JudyDoc™

Authorized attending-physician communication, structured clinical handoffs, professional review and coordination.

VERSION 3 · PLANNED

PatientRecall AI™

Secure integration to explain verified physician instructions, answer patient questions, issue reminders, and track follow-ups.

Clinical deployment requires more than a prototype: clinician-led evaluation, privacy and security engineering, accuracy and equity testing across languages and populations, appropriate consent, and medical-device regulatory review where applicable.
